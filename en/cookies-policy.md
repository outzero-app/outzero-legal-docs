# OUTZERO Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which types OUTZERO uses, and for what purposes they are used when the user accesses the mobile app or the website www.outzero.app.

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on the user's device (computer, mobile, or tablet) when visiting a website or using an application.

Their main purpose is to enable the proper functioning of the platform, remember user preferences, and obtain statistical information about service usage.

Cookies do not contain viruses, do not damage the device, and do not allow access to personal information stored on it.

## 2. What types of cookies does OUTZERO use?

OUTZERO uses only necessary and legitimate cookies and similar technologies, always in line with its Privacy Policy.

- a) Technical and necessary cookies: enable the basic functioning of the app and the web (session management, platform security, access to private areas, and service stability and performance).
- b) Preference or configuration cookies: remember language, region or general area, and basic preferences and filters. They improve the experience without identifying the user.
- c) Analytics and usage cookies: collect anonymous, aggregated information about app and web usage, interactions and technical performance, and error detection. They are used only to improve the service.
- d) Contextual advertising cookies: used to show promotional content based on region or approximate area and the content being viewed at that moment. OUTZERO does not use personalized advertising cookies or create individual commercial profiles.

## 3. Cookie management

The user can configure their browser or device to accept, reject, or delete cookies. Disabling certain cookies may affect service functionality.

## 4. Consent

By using the OUTZERO web or app, the user accepts the use of cookies in accordance with this policy. When legally required, the corresponding consent will be requested.

## 5. Updates

OUTZERO may update this Cookie Policy to adapt it to legal or technical changes. Users are encouraged to review it periodically.
