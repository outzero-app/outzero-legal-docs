# OUTZERO Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies are, which types OUTZERO uses, and for what purposes they are used when the user accesses the mobile app or the website www.outzero.app.

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on the user's device (computer, mobile, or tablet) when visiting a website. In mobile applications, equivalent technologies may also be used, such as local storage, SDK identifiers, or similar files that allow the service to work properly or to remember preferences.

Their main purpose is to enable the proper functioning of the platform, remember user preferences, and obtain statistical information about service usage.

Cookies do not contain viruses, do not damage the device, and do not allow access to personal information stored on it.

## 2. What types of cookies or similar technologies does OUTZERO use?

OUTZERO may use necessary cookies and similar technologies and, where legally permitted or after the corresponding consent, preference, analytics, and measurement cookies or identifiers, always in line with its Privacy Policy.

- a) Technical and necessary cookies or identifiers: enable the basic functioning of the app and the web, such as session management, platform security, access to private areas, storing consent choices, and service stability and performance.
- b) Preference or configuration cookies or identifiers: remember language, region or general area, and basic preferences and filters. They improve the experience without directly identifying the user.
- c) Analytics and usage cookies or identifiers: collect information about app and web usage, such as screen views, searches, content selections, usage flows, interactions, and limited technical performance context. They are used to understand how the service is used and to improve it.
- d) Performance or diagnostics identifiers: used to measure technical readiness, detect failures, and improve service stability and reliability.
- e) Contextual advertising cookies or identifiers: used to show promotional or sponsored content based on region or approximate area, placement, app context, or the content being viewed at that moment. OUTZERO does not use these technologies to create its own individualized advertising profiles.

## 3. Cookie management

The user can configure their browser or device to accept, reject, or delete cookies and similar technologies, when that option is available. Disabling certain cookies or identifiers may affect service functionality.

## 4. Consent

On the website, when legally required, OUTZERO will request consent before activating analytics, measurement, or other non-essential cookies or similar identifiers. The user may accept, reject, or later modify their choice through the cookie banner or configuration tools made available by OUTZERO. Technical cookies remain enabled because they are necessary for the operation of the service.

In the mobile app, some equivalent local storage or SDK identifiers may be used for technical operation, preferences, diagnostics, analytics, or advertising features depending on the platform, SDK, and legal basis that applies in each case.

## 5. Updates

OUTZERO may update this Cookie Policy to adapt it to legal or technical changes. Users are encouraged to review it periodically.
